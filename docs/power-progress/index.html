A BETTER SCORE THAN ONE BIG NUMBER

Read the energy boundary.

Average net electricity · MW

Generated electricity minus internal loads, integrated over the modeled pulse and dwell, then multiplied by assumed 80% availability. Additional unscheduled-outage energy is not included.

Burn net electricity · MW

The modeled net electrical output while the fusion burn is sustained. It is not continuous plant output over the whole operating cycle.

Fusion yield · MJ

Reaction energy from a shot or pulse. It is not electrical energy. Converting yield to mean power requires a matching time interval; dividing by a laser peak or unrelated duration is invalid.

Gain at a stated boundary

Laser-on-target gain and plasma heating gain are different from whole-facility or net-electric gain. A value above one at a target does not establish positive electricity to the grid.
