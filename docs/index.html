What works—and what does not

Software verification, conditional model results and experimental support are different evidence levels. Passing tests do not certify a reactor.

The existing OKTAVIAN lithium benchmark closely reproduces the archived calculation, while the primary neutron table and a legacy first-bin conversion expose unresolved differences. Current research decision

The cooling hardware cannot be approved from hydraulics alone.

The latest candidate-linked local neutron calculation predicts about 2.71% lower tritium production with front headers than with rear headers. This is a conditional local design comparison—not the complete reactor’s breeding ratio, recovered fuel, or an electricity improvement.

Its source geometry is a declared proxy and differs by 2.407% in plasma volume from the systems model. The next gate is compatible breeder-benchmark evidence and a matched full-geometry source/material/return-flux calculation.

Technical values and calculation details
Why are we testing this?
The pipes remove heat. But their position also changes what happens to neutrons in the surrounding fuel-producing material. We are comparing layouts before accepting a design.
Is this the whole reactor?
No. This is a simplified local section linked to our r838 reference concept. It is not the later higher-power alternative, and it does not prove complete reactor performance.
What do the colors mean?
Cyan moving dots are neutrons; yellow dots are photons. Lines mark their calculated paths. The dots are intentionally much bigger and slower than real particles.
Where is the heat view?
Turn on Heating slice to see where the calculation deposits energy. It is not a temperature or coolant-flow measurement. Click the slice for details.
Heating values appear here when you select a point on the slice.
Scientific limits
Recorded independent particle histories are aligned at emission for playback. Source assumptions, reflecting side boundaries, simplified materials and local geometry remain limitations. No whole-device fuel-production ratio, measured radiation field or electrical gain is established.



Recorded histories are not a live plasma. Viewing does not modify an accepted design or launch a solver. No global tritium-breeding ratio or higher plant output is claimed.
