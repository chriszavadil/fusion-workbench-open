What works—and what does not

Software verification, conditional model results and experimental support are different evidence levels. Passing tests do not certify a reactor.

Latest validation checkpoint

A reproduced calculation is not the same as a validated measurement.

The existing OKTAVIAN lithium benchmark closely reproduces the archived calculation, while the primary neutron table and a legacy first-bin conversion expose unresolved differences. Current research decision

The cooling hardware cannot be approved from hydraulics alone.

The latest candidate-linked local neutron calculation predicts about 2.71% lower tritium production with front headers than with rear headers. This is a conditional local design comparison—not the complete reactor’s breeding ratio, recovered fuel, or an electricity improvement.

Its source geometry is a declared proxy and differs by 2.407% in plasma volume from the systems model. The next gate is compatible breeder-benchmark evidence and a matched full-geometry source/material/return-flux calculation.

What is available in this preview

Two dimension-linked device envelopes and their recorded electrical profiles are interactive. Earlier control, materials, fuel and equipment work is catalogued by research area; complete historical run playback and machine-specific scenes are not yet integrated.

Neither display model includes an experimentally validated plasma equilibrium, matched neutron heat map, or manufactured blanket design. Results are not safety or operating guidance.

Physical quantities
Cyan markers: neutrons. Gold markers: photons. Positions, times and energies come from recorded OpenMC histories.
Particle size, glow and playback speed are visualization choices. They do not indicate particle size, source strength or a measured radiation field.
Heating, not temperature
The slice shows deposited energy per unit volume per source neutron. A thermal solver and source normalization are still required to infer operating temperature.
Evidence boundary
Both incidence assumptions retain lower local yield for front headers. Their quantitative interaction is not resolved at 99% Monte Carlo confidence. Neither assumption establishes the real reactor source.
