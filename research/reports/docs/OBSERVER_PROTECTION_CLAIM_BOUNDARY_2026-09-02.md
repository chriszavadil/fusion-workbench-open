# Observer-protection claim boundary

The active research target is a protection-level layer: given diagnostic history, known commands, model/output-map discrepancy, bounded profile evolution, latency, and PF voltage/slew/shared-authority constraints, determine whether worst-case vertical ambiguity is small enough to certify a recovery decision.

This project does not claim novelty for vertical-position control, passive-current modelling, Kalman filtering, H-infinity observers, unknown-input observers, maximum controllable displacement, watchdog logic, MPC, or reference governors. A publishable increment would require a prospective machine-derived or experimental result that jointly treats those uncertainties and changes a diagnostic, estimator, PF-power, latency, or availability decision.

A software-model result is not experimental MAST/MAST-U validation, a tokamak operating limit, controller certification, ITER evidence, reactor-safety evidence, net energy, or sustainable fusion.
