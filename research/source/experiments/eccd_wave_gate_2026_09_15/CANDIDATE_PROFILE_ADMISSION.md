## Physics boundaries
A systems-model elongation/triangularity plus Ip and total pressure does not uniquely determine a magnetic equilibrium or radial current profile. A fixed-boundary force-balanced proxy will need separately declared profile and coordinate choices; no borrowed ITER equilibrium or geometric torus is relabeled as the candidate. A missing pressure/profile quantity is not replaced by an arbitrary value. The subsequent wave test must distinguish launched, absorbed and wall-plug power, current direction, radial deposition and the residual control requirement. Even success on a proxy does not demonstrate stability or validate the higher plant output.
