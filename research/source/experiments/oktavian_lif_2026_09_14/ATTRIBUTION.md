# Attribution and reuse scope: OKTAVIAN lithium-fluoride benchmark

This is a reproduction and data-interface audit of existing research, not a new experiment or a claimed fusion discovery. No affiliation with, endorsement by, or independent review from the original researchers, IAEA, MIT or JADE is implied.

**Original measurements:** Chihiro Ichihara, Shu A. Hayashi, Katsuhei Kobayashi, Itsuro Kimura, Junji Yamamoto, Mikio Izumi and Akito Takahashi, *The measurement of leakage neutron spectra from various sphere piles with 14 MeV neutrons*, Nuclear Data for Science and Technology (1988). The authors' abstract explicitly includes LiF. We read that abstract, not the full proceedings paper: https://wwwndc.jaea.go.jp/nd1988/abstracts/12916-0319.html . The OFB archive labels its measurement record 9 July 1987; the proceedings attribution is 1988, not a claim that we dated the actual experiment independently.

**Measurement tables and MCNP input:** IAEA Nuclear Data Section, `IAEA-NDS/open-benchmarks`, revision `09bacd7927b581d5e0f90468a0599fec8e95f972`, under **CC BY 4.0**. Exact source paths and hashes are in `UPSTREAM_MANIFEST.json`; the unchanged license is `upstream/IAEA_LICENSE`. Source: https://github.com/IAEA-NDS/open-benchmarks/tree/09bacd7927b581d5e0f90468a0599fec8e95f972 . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Tables, error values and input are preserved unchanged; our derived bin integrals, ratios, reformatting and explanatory labels are identified as our analysis, not modifications approved by the data owners. The IAEA repository notes that some material originates in CoNDERC/SINBAD and may be adapted for JADE.

**OpenMC model, helper and archived results:** OpenMC Fusion Benchmarks contributors; copyright (c) 2025 MIT PSFC, **MIT license**, repository `eepeterson/openmc_fusion_benchmarks`, revision `c47fc573a7e2a9ca11a8958dc67155b1146adf51`. Source: https://github.com/eepeterson/openmc_fusion_benchmarks/tree/c47fc573a7e2a9ca11a8958dc67155b1146adf51 . The unchanged upstream notice is `upstream/OFB_LICENSE`. The original model's comment calls this a tungsten experiment; the actual LiF composition, directory and geometry establish the selected model. We did not edit the upstream source to hide that comment. The archived computed dataset identifies OpenMC 0.14.0, ENDF/B-8.0, September 2024, MIT-PSFC. Our run uses OpenMC 0.15.2 and independently recorded nuclear-data file hashes; matching library labels alone is not proof of identical processed files.

**Framework and benchmark interpretation:** JADE documentation explains the existing benchmark and the more directly relevant FNG HCPB tritium-production benchmark: https://jade-a-nuclear-data-libraries-vv-tool.readthedocs.io/en/latest/benchmarks/experimental.html . Davide Laghi et al., *JADE, a new software tool for nuclear fusion data libraries verification & validation*, Fusion Engineering and Design 161 (2020) 112075, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fusengdes.2020.112075 . We used documentation for scope, not JADE execution or copied GPL source.

**Our contribution in this packet:** bounded local replay, frozen file hashes, explicit normalization comparison, handling of unscored bins, and a data-linked public visualization. Original analysis/UI code is MIT licensed; that does not relabel the IAEA tables or grant new rights over third-party material. All retained files are explicitly listed in the manifests. No third-party paper PDF, credential, private email or raw execution log is bundled.
